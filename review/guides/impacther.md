# Reviewing ImpactHer (HackYeah 2026 open task, 8,000 PLN)

## What the organizer asked for
A technology solution for a real challenge that disproportionately affects women: safety, health, education, career, access to services, digital participation. Start from a well-identified need and show practical, measurable impact. Typically a thinner field — judged seriously, not as a theme variant.

## How to read each criterion here
- **Idea & Innovation (30)**: insight into the actual need (safety routing with real incident data, return-to-work retraining, menopause or endometriosis tracking gaps in healthcare). Points for naming a specific life situation, not "women's app".
- **Relation to Category (20)**: the women-specific need is explicit in the product's function, not just naming and pink UI. If the product is gender-neutral with a label, relation caps at 5.
- **Practical Applicability / Usability (20)**: reachable by the affected group; safety features must be discreet, health features must be accurate; Polish context is an asset.
- **Design (20)**: dignity and accessibility; dark, calm patterns for safety tools.
- **Completeness & Implementation (10)**: the core helping loop works; for safety, the emergency path is real.

## Evidence checklist
- The need is evidenced: research citations, statistics, the target group's own words in the deck.
- A measurable-impact plan: what number gets better, by how much, measured how.
- For safety: a working alert/escape flow, not only a map.
- Data sensitivity acknowledged (health or location data of a vulnerable group).

## Verification steps
1. Strip the naming: does any feature only make sense for this need?
2. Find the impact measurement in the deck or docs; no plan caps the idea score.
3. Check the sensitive-data story — silence is a weakness here.

## Weak-entry patterns
- Generic tracker rebranded with a women's name.
- Impact claimed ("empowers 51% of population") with nothing measured.
- Safety feature that would alert the attacker (bad UX reveals the gap in thinking).
